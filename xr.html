    In the headset

    • Turn pinch or grip the globe and move your hand; let go mid-swing to spin it.
    • Resize hold the globe with both hands and pull apart or together.
    • Move pinch the glass rim of the base; two hands on the rim turn it.
    • Inspect tap a contact with a quick pinch, or aim and tap from a distance.
    • Place in passthrough, look at a table and pinch to set the globe on it.
    • Tabletop map switch from the console or the button above: the map opens on the part of the globe facing you, or on the selected contact. Pinch to pan, two hands to zoom and turn.
